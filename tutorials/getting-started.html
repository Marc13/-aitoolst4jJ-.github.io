Home Projects Tutorials

Step 1: Pick one tool

Choose a single tool (e.g., ChatGPT or Canva) and commit to 15 minutes/day for a week.

Step 2: One tiny task

Write a caption, summarize a paragraph, or rename files. Keep it small and repeatable.

Step 3: Save your wins

Create a notes page for prompts, results, and lessons. Progress beats perfection.
